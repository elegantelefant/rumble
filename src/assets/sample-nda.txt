Effective date: 1 October 2026
Parties: Alder Quay Analytics Ltd. ("Alder") and Bracken Lantern Systems Ltd.
("Bracken"), together the "Parties".

1. Purpose
The Parties wish to evaluate a potential collaboration to develop inventory
forecasting software (the "Purpose"). Each Party may disclose information to
the other in connection with the Purpose.

2. Confidential Information
"Confidential Information" means any technical, commercial, financial, or
other information disclosed by or on behalf of a Party (the "Discloser") to
the other Party (the "Recipient"), whether in writing, orally, visually, or
electronically, including source code, customer lists, pricing, prototypes,
and the existence and terms of the discussions. Information need not be
marked confidential to receive protection under this Agreement.

3. Recipient Obligations
The Recipient shall use Confidential Information only for the Purpose and
protect it with reasonable care, no less than the care used for its own
confidential information. The Recipient may disclose it to employees,
contractors, and professional advisers who need to know for the Purpose
and are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as protective as
these terms. The Recipient is responsible for their compliance and shall
promptly notify the Discloser of any unauthorized use or disclosure.

4. Exclusions and Required Disclosure
These obligations do not apply to information the Recipient can demonstrate
was publicly available without breach, was lawfully known before disclosure,
or was lawfully obtained from a third party without a duty of confidence.
If disclosure is required by law or court order, the Recipient shall, where
lawful, give advance notice and reasonable assistance in seeking protection,
and disclose only the portion legally required.

5. Ownership and Disclaimer
All Confidential Information remains the Discloser's property. No license or
other intellectual property right is granted except the limited right to use
the information for the Purpose. Information is provided "as is", without
warranty of accuracy or completeness. Neither Party must proceed with a
transaction, and any commercial arrangement requires a separate agreement.

6. Return and Destruction
Within ten business days after written request, the Recipient shall return
or destroy all Confidential Information and copies and certify completion.
One archival copy may be retained to satisfy legal obligations, subject to
the continuing confidentiality obligations of this Agreement.

7. Term and Survival
This Agreement continues for two years from the effective date unless either
Party terminates it on thirty days' written notice. Confidentiality and use
restrictions survive termination for five years from each disclosure.

8. Remedies and Liability
The Parties acknowledge that unauthorized disclosure may cause irreparable
harm and that the Discloser may seek injunctive relief in addition to other
available remedies. The Recipient shall indemnify the Discloser against all
losses, costs, and expenses arising from a breach of this Agreement.

This Agreement is governed by the laws of England and Wales, whose courts
have exclusive jurisdiction. Notices shall be sent to each Party's designated

Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the other's written consent.
